# Extras Curriculum Research Record

## What changed
The Extras area is now being treated as a real teacher-planning library. Each pathway in `extra-curriculum.js` contains six starter sessions. Every session states an observable learner outcome, a low-cost activity, and evidence Eddy can keep in a club or inspection folder. Each pathway also includes an age band, practical skills, safety boundaries, source links and a caveat.

The content is deliberately framed as **teacher-designed optional enrichment**. Official sources inform the skills and progression, but they do not approve this exact six-session sequence, make it compulsory, or award a certificate for completing it.

## Product boundary
- Source links are shown to Eddy so he can read the underlying documents.
- The app does not invent official strand codes, content standards or indicators for extracurricular clubs.
- The school’s approved curriculum, timetable, safeguarding rules and qualified staff remain the final authority.
- Practical activities are intentionally low-cost and paper-first, with age-appropriate alternatives for limited equipment or no internet.
- Sewing, electronics, solar, food and construction activities include conservative safety limits. The starter pathways exclude mains electrical work, hazardous e-waste dismantling, flames, chemicals, powered workshop tools and unsupervised off-site activities.
- Completing a pathway inside Benni does not create a CTVET certificate or replace formal vocational training.
